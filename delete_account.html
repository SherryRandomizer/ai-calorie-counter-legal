Delete Your Account

AI Calorie Counter — Last updated: October 3, 2026

You can permanently delete your AI Calorie Counter account and all data associated with it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open AI Calorie Counter and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, then scroll to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the warning, then enter your password when asked.

Your account and data are deleted immediately.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, email us from the address registered to your account:

shoaibjamil85@gmail.com — subject: "Account deletion request"

We will delete your account and data within 30 days of receiving your request.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in credentials)
  • Your profile, goals, and settings
  • All logged meals and meal notes
  • Weight history and body measurements
  • Water intake, fasting history, and supplement logs
  • Favorites, saved recipes, planned meals, and reminders

What we do not store

Photos you take or upload for AI analysis are not stored in your account, so there are no photos to delete. Anonymous crash and usage diagnostics are not linked to your account and may be retained.

See our Privacy Policy for details on how your data is handled.
